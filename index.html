About

Built for the messy middle between idea and product.

I am Ginej Neupane, better known as GrimNej in the developer community. I am a Bachelor of Computer Application student from Nepal focused on AI, cloud computing, scalable deployment, and open source engineering.

My strongest mode is rapid execution. I can take an unclear concept, map the architecture, identify the technical risk, and produce a functional prototype or MVP quickly enough to make the next decision obvious.

I work across stacks instead of forcing one. Python, Rust, TypeScript, Docker, Linux, cloud APIs, LLM workflows, and strict automation are tools in the same kit: choose the right pieces, make the system understandable, then ship.
